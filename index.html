Why PP is Sustainable ?

Polypropylene (PP) is more sustainable than most other food-packaging materials such as bagasse, paper, PLA-bioplastics, or aluminium because it is lightweight, reusable, recyclable, and requires far less water and energy during production. In the Indian food ecosystem, most dishes are gravy-based, oily, or liquid in nature, and only PP containers can reliably hold hot curries and gravies without leaking, softening, or breaking down — which is a major drawback for bagasse, paper and PLA. Since PP can be reused multiple times while maintaining hygiene and strength, its carbon footprint per use becomes much lower than single-use “eco” alternatives, and it also fits well into India’s existing recycling network. This makes PP one of the most practical and truly sustainable options for real-world Indian food packaging.

SCREEN PRINTING

Screen printing is one the most common print technology in plastic container decoration. It’s a technique used to apply information such as size, weight & volume, brand details, durability & legibility, nutritional details, expiration and production dates, symbols and storage instructions onto the surface of plastic packaging such as bottles, jars, jerrycans and buckets.​
